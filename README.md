# OrderSphere

An Enterprise B2B Order-to-Cash platform connecting buyers, suppliers and enterprise business workflows.

## Product Vision

To manage the flow from the supply of initial products to combining individual products into a complete product and selling it to buyers.

## Business Problem

This product aims to solve business problems by:
- Handling business operations efficiently.
- Finding the position and status of each workflow.
- Identifying where issues occur and understanding their negative impacts.
- Understanding how to improve business profitability.
- Generating business reports.

## Target Users

An enterprise business platform that companies can use to track their work, business operations and output.

## Initial Business Workflows

Initially, the workflow starts with receiving products from suppliers, combining products into a complete product, and selling them to buyers based on quotations and other business processes.
